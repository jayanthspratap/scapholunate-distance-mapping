How to read it

Each coloured block is one measurement: the radial-ulnar gap from scaphoid to lunate across one image row of one coronal CT slice, the same direction a PA radiograph measures. Its position is where it was measured; its colour is its width.

Only image rows within 1 mm of each slice's narrowest gap are kept (up to 7 mm), so the blocks cover the part of the joint where the bones face each other.

Positions are measured from the ulnar margin of the scaphoid (its edge facing the lunate) on the coronal slice where the scaphoid is largest. All wrists are displayed in one orientation, scaphoid to the left when viewed from dorsal; left and right wrists are not distinguished.

Each tile is the mean SLD of the 25 wrists at one point of the 7 × 7 mm grid used in the paper, at 1 mm steps. The grid is centred on the ulnar margin of the scaphoid (its edge facing the lunate) on the coronal slice where the scaphoid is largest. Each wrist contributes its measurement nearest to each grid point; tiles are not interpolated.

Diamonds mark, for each wrist, the CT point whose SLD is closest to its PA radiograph. Some lie outside the grid. Point at one to see its wrist; click to open it.
